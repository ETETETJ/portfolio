Entong Jiang is a designer and artist from Shanghai, China, currently based in The Hague, the Netherlands.

With a background in product design in Shanghai, her practice moves between design and art.
She uses 3D modeling to develop visual ideas and turns digital designs into physical objects through 3D printing. She also works with 2D and 3D animation, creative short films, and electronic installations.

Her practice explores themes of the unconscious, identity, female identity, and broader global concerns.
By connecting abstract ideas with tangible forms, she creates narratives that are playful, humorous, and subtly satirical, inviting the audience to experience familiar subjects from unexpected perspectives.
