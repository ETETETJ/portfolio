Concept

Utopia - Seedan

In an era of severe seed homogenization, there exists a utopia called Seedan.

Here, we believe:
Currency is seed, seed is currency. Value and nourishment are no longer separate.

Every person carries a seed, and our responsibility is to keep it alive -- by exchanging, cultivating, and cross-breeding it with others, to create new food culture and share their stories with the world.

We encourage openness, collaboration, and experimentation, allowing seeds to give birth to new forms of matter and energy.

We advocate for a world where everyone can plant, harvest, and shhare -- no longer a privilege reserved for the few.
