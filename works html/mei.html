Me & I # graphic design, photography, writing, 2025

postcards

In my twenties, I often feel as if two different souls live inside me.
One is in charge of my body’s inner world -- the rhythm of health, energy, and survival.
The other runs my thoughts, choices, and how i connect with people outside.
They’re separate yet inseparable, coexisting in the same body without speaking to each other.
Together they stumble forward through the uncertainty and chaos of my twenties.
