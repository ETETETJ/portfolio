Stranger in Unkown A film created for the orchestral piece An American in Paris. # storyboard design, acting, scene setup, object making, 3d animtion(blender), 2025

An Asian arrives in a Western city, moving from excitement to inner conflict, then homesickness, gradual assimilation, and finally a renewed sense of pleasure.

We use symboloc objects from both Eastern and Western cultures to mirror shifting identities and emotions as the music evolves.

These cultural elements colide, blend, and reshape one another, echoing the narrative arc of ‘An American in Paris’.

In the end, the Eastern identity -- symbolized by boba -- ‘integrates’ into the Western world.
